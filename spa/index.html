The cost of the marginal unit SRMC national · spark zonal

SRMC = (gas + CO₂) ÷ efficiency. The clean spark spread — power price minus SRMC — is what a gas plant keeps after paying for fuel and carbon: the floor under the power price, and which cost — gas or carbon — moved it. Gas and carbon are one national cost; the power price, and so the spread, is the selected zone's.
